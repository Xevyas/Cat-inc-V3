# Story Dialogue Guide

This document defines the voice and continuity rules for Cat Inc stories. Story dialogue is stored in `index.html`; dynamic speaker names and story replay behavior are handled in the Story Modals section of `jeu.js`.

## General tone

- Stories are written in natural English with light, offbeat humor.
- The cats are intelligent and organized, but they still behave like cats. Warm surfaces, boxes, food, naps, smells, tails, birds and pawprints should remain part of their reasoning.
- Humor should come from character reactions and feline behavior rather than constant cat puns.
- Do not use em dashes in narration or dialogue.
- Avoid repetitive monologues. Let another available character react when it improves the rhythm.
- When humans are present, cats speak through `Miaou` lines with the English meaning in parentheses. Once the humans have left, the player reads the cats' conversation directly.

## Bernardo

Bernardo is the confident and friendly Gang Leader. He is clever, opportunistic and slightly devious, especially when dealing with humans, but he genuinely wants the Gang to succeed.

- He presents appropriation as borrowing, redistribution, recovered assets or responsible use of abandoned materials.
- His normal dialogue uses complete, naturally flowing sentences.
- Avoid turning his speeches into a sequence of slogans, fragments or clauses separated by excessive punctuation.
- Short sentences are reserved for orders, hunting, a deliberate punchline or emphasis.
- He accepts a sensible objection from Luna without losing authority.

## Mochi

Mochi is Bernardo's enthusiastic companion and supports his plans, sometimes before fully understanding them.

- Mochi is sincere, literal, optimistic and easily distracted.
- Food and naps are recurring interests, but should not be the only source of humor.
- Mochi may misunderstand a complicated term in an innocent way.
- Mochi is never cynical or deliberately cruel.

## Luna

Luna is calmer and more observant than the others. Her humor is dry, and she gently exposes the weaknesses in Bernardo's plans or Mochi's reasoning.

- Her remarks are often shorter than Bernardo's explanations.
- She teases Mochi most often, but Bernardo is not immune to a well-placed remark.
- She remains loyal and useful. Her teasing must not make her hostile or dismissive of the Gang.
- She may propose the practical correction after making a joke.

## Continuity constraints

- Luna cannot speak before she joins as the third Cat.
- `A New Horizon` uses the dynamically trained Explorator's name and portrait. Its dialogue must work for Mochi, Luna or a later recruited Cat.
- `Chef's kiss` uses the dynamically assigned cook. The cook must not address Bernardo as `boss`, because Bernardo himself can produce the first Catnip Salad.
- Human evacuation scenes retain the human dialogue before switching to the trio's reaction.
- Gameplay explanations in orange narration remain factual and must stay synchronized with the actual mechanic.
